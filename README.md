## About

I am a medical student and bioinformatics thesis student working at the intersection of **clinical medicine, computational biology and reproducible research software**. My current program studies non-genetic therapy resistance, spatial drug transport, tumor microenvironments and low-cost diagnostic AI in solid tumors.

I build small, auditable tools that can be inspected, tested and reused: Python CLIs, spatial-omics assays, Nextflow modules, package recipes, interactive research portals and educational clinical software.

**Current focus — November 2026:** bioinformatics thesis work at TIGEM, computational oncology research, reproducible spatial-omics software and open-source scientific infrastructure.

🏰 **Personal research hub:** [Caverna di Luca](https://lukecele-research.vercel.app/) — portfolio, live demos and selected frames.

## Clinical education

### [MedTerminal Cardio-Toracico](https://github.com/Lukecele/cardio-thoracic-medterminal)

An interactive learning platform for medical students, built with React, TypeScript, Three.js and Tailwind CSS:

- 3D cardiac and thoracic anatomy;
- simulated ECG telemetry and acute STEMI/NSTEMI patterns;
- cardiac murmurs and respiratory sound library;
- case-based active recall and bedside calculators.
