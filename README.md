## 🔬 Featured Scientific Research: Spatial Oncology & Biophysics

A comprehensive independent open-source research initiative on **non-genetic targeted therapy resistance, subcellular spatial transcriptomics, and low-cost digital pathology AI** for EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). Developed in affiliation with **Department of Clinical Medicine and Surgery, Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II** & experimental degree thesis in bioinformatics at **TIGEM** (Telethon Institute of Genetics and Medicine).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **🌟 [Master Study: Spatial Oncology & Biophysical Sanctuary Dynamics](https://github.com/Lukecele/spatial-oncology-master)**  
> **Unified Multi-Scale Framework:** Integrates physical transport, evolutionary game theory, clinical cohort survival, and digital pathology AI into an unbroken causal continuum:  
> • **Biophysical Reaction-Diffusion:** Exact modified Bessel order-zero ($K_0$) radial decay ($\lambda = 627.7\,\mu\text{m}$, $r_{\text{crit}} = 165.9\,\mu\text{m}$, viable sanctuary shell $\Delta r = 34.1\,\mu\text{m}$ at $\theta = 0.35$).  
> • **Evolutionary Game Theory:** Frequency-dependent replicator dynamics proving asymptotic Lyapunov stability of the DTP coexistence niche ($x^* = 0.6133$, $\lambda_{\text{eig}} = -1.7934 < 0$) with calibrated post-ADAURA cessation regrowth (ctDNA detection at 10.7 mo, CT relapse at 16.1 mo, lead time 5.4 mo).  
> • **Clinical Cohort Validation:** MSKCC 2020 cohort ($N = 604$), demonstrating an extreme biophysical contrast of $HR = 6.75$ ($95\%\text{ CI: } 2.28 - 19.97, p = 5.52 \times 10^{-4}$) between solid/micropapillary architecture and lepidic baseline ($N=193$).  
> • **Routine Diagnostic AI:** Attention-MIL neural network predicting microvascular decoupling and osimertinib PFS directly from routine H&E whole-slide images ($< 5\text{ €}$).
