## 🔬 Scientific Research: Computational Oncology Program

Independent medical student research program investigating **non-genetic targeted therapy resistance, subcellular biophysical transport, and low-cost diagnostic AI** in *EGFR*-mutant lung adenocarcinoma (NSCLC). Affiliated with the Department of Clinical Medicine and Surgery, School of Medicine, **Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II**, Naples, Italy.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### 👑 [Grand Unified Framework: The Spatial-Genomic Oncology Continuum](https://github.com/Lukecele/spatial-continuum-oncology)
> **A Biophysical Reaction-Diffusion Theory of Pharmacological Sanctuaries, the Post-ADAURA Cessation Dilemma, and the Bedside Composite Resistance Index (CRI)**  
> *Target: Nature Communications* | *Preprint under screening: medRxiv (MEDRXIV/2026/366778)* | *Live Preprint: Research Square ([10.21203/rs.3.rs-11530312/v1](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11530312/v1))*  
> 1. **Biophysical Reaction-Diffusion Transport:** Cylindrical modified Bessel $K_0$ decay establishing that an outward microvascular cord develops a sub-therapeutic pharmacological sanctuary shell ($\Delta r = 34.1\,\mu\text{m}$, $31.3\%$ cord volume) between $r_{\text{crit}} = 165.9\,\mu\text{m}$ and the hypoxia/necrosis boundary $r_{\text{ox}} = 200.0\,\mu\text{m}$.  
> 2. **Universal Regrowth-Kinetics Identity:** Proof that post-cessation ctDNA-to-CT imaging lead time is an invariant algebraic identity of exponential doubling ($\Delta t = T_s \log_2(100) \approx 5.46\,\text{months}$), explaining the post-ADAURA recurrence clustering without secondary gatekeeper mutations.  
> 3. **Clinical Translation & Bedside CRI:** Formulation of the Composite Resistance Index ($\text{CRI} = 0.40 \cdot \Delta r_{\text{norm}} + 0.35 \cdot \text{VDI}_{\text{norm}} + 0.25 \cdot \text{HR}_{\text{hist}}$) and the SG-ADAURA multi-center trial protocol.

## 🩺 Medical Platforms & Software Engineering

### 🩺 [MedTerminal Cardio-Toracico](https://github.com/Lukecele/cardio-thoracic-medterminal)
Interactive multimodal educational platform for medical students at Università degli Studi di Napoli Federico II: 3D WebGL cardiac/thoracic anatomy (Three.js), simulated ECG telemetry, remastered heart & lung auscultation audio, active recall quizzes, and clinical calculators.  
`React 19` · `TypeScript` · `Three.js` · `Tailwind v4` · **[Explore live platform ↗](https://cardio-thoracic-medterminal.vercel.app)**
